PREAMBLE
WHEREAS the Platform operates an online marketplace dedicated to the creation, exhibition, promotion and sale of digital works of art, including, without limitation, NFTs (non-fungible tokens);
WHEREAS the Artist creates original digital works of art and wishes to distribute, promote and monetize these works through the Platform;
WHEREAS the parties wish to establish the general conditions governing this relationship;
THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual commitments and agreements contained herein, and in consideration of other valid and useful consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:


ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS
1.1 Defined terms
• The term “Platform” refers to the Marketplace, accessible at the address https://makeitart.io/, as well as all associated services, tools and functionalities.
• The term “Work(s)” means any original digital creative work submitted by the Artist to the Platform, including, without limitation, digital art, illustrations, photographs, 3D models, generative art and animations.
• “NFT” means a non-fungible token created on a blockchain network (Ethereum, Polygon or other) representing ownership or rights to a Work of Art.
• “Primary Sale” means the first sale of a Work of Art or an NFT directly by the Artist via the Platform.
• “Secondary sale” means any subsequent resale of a Work of Art or an NFT by a buyer on the secondary market.
• “Gross Sale Price” means the total amount paid by a buyer for an Artwork or NFT, excluding blockchain gas fees.
• “Commission” means the fees levied by the Platform on Primary Sales, as defined in Article 7.
• “Royalty” means the percentage received by the Artist on Secondary Sales, as defined in article 7.


ARTICLE 2 – SCOPE OF APPLICATION OF THE CONTRACT
2.1 Independent relationship
This contract establishes an independent service provider relationship. The Artist is neither an employee, nor an agent, nor an associate, nor a joint venture partner of the Platform. The Artist retains full control over the manner and means of creating the Works of Art.
2.2 Non-exclusivity
This contract is non-exclusive. The Artist retains the right to: - sell Works of art via other platforms or channels; - create similar works for other purposes; - manage your own website or your own direct sales channels.
2.3 Selection of artists
Platform reserves the right to select artist applications based on artistic quality, suitability for the platform and compliance with these conditions. Platform may reject artist applications or artwork submissions in its reasonable discretion, but such discretion shall not be exercised in a discriminatory manner or in bad faith.

The Artist retains all copyright and intellectual property rights in the Works. This Agreement does not constitute a transfer of copyright ownership.

The Platform provides standardized licensing models, but the Artist is responsible for setting appropriate terms.

In the event of termination of this Contract: - the Artist may request the withdrawal of unsold Works of Art within 30 days; - the Platform retains limited archiving rights on the Works of art sold in order to preserve records of transactions; - the licenses granted to the Buyer remain valid and enforceable.
4.6 Moral rights
To the extent authorized by law, the Artist waives the right to assert his moral rights against the Platform for any reasonable promotional adaptation (cropping, color adjustment, format conversion) carried out in good faith. The Artist retains all other moral rights.

ARTICLE 5 – PRICING AND CONDITIONS OF SALE
5.1 Pricing power
The Artist has exclusive discretionary power to set: - the initial sales prices of the Works; - royalty percentages on secondary sales (within the limits of the Platform parameters); - print runs and release schedules; - the types of licenses offered (personal, commercial, exclusive).
5.2 Pricing Recommendations
The Platform may provide pricing recommendations based on market data, but may not impose specific prices, except to: - enforce minimum technical requirements (for example, a minimum bid to cover gas costs); - prevent obvious pricing errors or fraudulent advertisements; - preserve the integrity of the Platform (for example, by preventing €0.01 ads aimed at manipulating rankings).
5.3 Price changes
The Artist may change the prices of unsold Works of Art at any time. Works of art sold are subject to the purchase conditions in force at the time of the sale.
5.4 Sales process
The Platform acts solely as a technical intermediary facilitating transactions between the Artist and buyers. The Platform: - processes payments via secure third-party providers; - facilitates the minting of NFTs and transactions on the blockchain; - provides escrow services for transaction security; - delivers digital files to buyers.


ARTICLE 6 – REMUNERATION AND PAYMENT CONDITIONS
6.1 Commission structure
Commission on primary sales:
The Platform charges a 7% commission on the gross sales price of all primary sales.
IMPORTANT: this rate is fixed for the first 12 months of this contract. After 12 months, the Platform may adjust the rates upon 90 days written notice to the Artist. Rate increases are capped at 2% per year and cannot exceed 10% in total without the written consent of the Artist.
6.2 Royalties on secondary sales
Artist's royalties: the Artist himself sets the percentage of his royalties on secondary sales, between 5% and 15% of the resale price.
Platform Fees on Secondary Sales: The Platform may charge up to 2.5% on secondary sales to cover costs related to blockchain infrastructure.
Smart contract enforcement: Royalties on secondary sales are enforced via smart contracts on the blockchain where technically feasible. The Platform uses commercially reasonable efforts to ensure compliance with fees, but cannot guarantee their application on all third-party marketplaces.
6.3 Payment processing
Payment methods accepted: - Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) - Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) - Cryptocurrencies (ETH, MATIC, USDC) - ACH/bank transfer (for large transactions)
Payment processing fees: Third-party payment provider fees (typically 2.9% + ≃ €0.30 for card payments) are deducted from the gross sales price before commission calculation.

Calculation example:
Sale price of the work: €1,000.00
Payment processing fee (2.9% + €0.30): -€29.30
Net amount of sale: €970.70
Platform Commission (7%): -€67.95
Payment paid to the artist: €902.75
6.4 Payment schedule
Standard payment: payments to artists are made weekly (every Friday) via the payment method chosen by the artist: - ACH transfer (US bank accounts): no fees - PayPal: 2% fee (capped at €20) - Cryptocurrency: transaction fee at cost - International transfer: flat fee of €25
Minimum payment threshold: equivalent to €50 EU. Balances below this threshold carry over to the next payment period.
Payment Schedule: - Week 1: Sales occur Monday through Sunday - Week 2: 7-day hold period for fraud prevention purposes - Week 3: Payment processed on Friday
Expedited payment option: available for a fee of €10 (next business day, minimum of €100).
6.5 Blockchain and gas fees
Minting costs of NFTs: - Delayed minting (default): gas fees paid by the buyer at the time of the first sale - Pre-minting option: the artist can pre-mint at his own expense
Supported by the Platform: the Platform supports gas costs for: - platform smart contract deployments - royalty distribution contracts - creation of collection contracts
Artist Responsibility: The artist covers gas fees for: - Optional pre-minting of NFTs - Transfers to external wallets - Direct interactions with the blockchain outside the platform
6.6 Refunds and Chargebacks
Refund policy: - Digital downloads: no refund after download, unless the item differs significantly from its description - NFT: no refund after transfer to the blockchain, except in cases of fraud or misrepresentation - Technical breakdowns: full refund if the Platform cannot fulfill the order
Chargeback Protection: The Platform manages chargebacks and fraud claims. The artist's payment will NOT be canceled, except in cases of proven fraud and complicity of the artist.
6.7 Financial transparency
The Platform provides the artist: - A sales dashboard - Monthly financial statements - number of subscribers gained

ARTICLE 8 – PROTECTION AND SAFETY OF WORKS
8.1 Copyright protection measures
The Platform implements:
(a) Digital tailor: watermarks visible on preview images (artist can customize them)
(b) Invisible forensic marking: tracking data embedded in files
(c) DMCA takedown procedure: simplified system for respecting copyright
(d) Right-click protection: disabled on display pages (not foolproof, but deters casual copying)
(e) Content ID analysis: automated detection of unauthorized copies (where this service is available)
8.2 Data security
• Encryption: AES-256 encryption for stored files
• Secure transmission: TLS 1.3 for all data transfers
• Access controls: role-based authorizations
• Regular audits: annual security audits carried out by a third party
8.3 DMCA and responding to infringement reports
Obligations of the artist: - Respond to notifications of violation within 5 working days - File a counter-notification if the complaint is unfounded - Compensate the Platform in the event of abusive withdrawal claims
Measures taken by the Platform: - Transmit violation notifications to the artist within 48 hours - Temporarily deactivate the contested content pending resolution of the dispute - Restore the content upon receipt of a valid counter-notification - Terminate the account of repeat infringers (three strikes policy)

ARTICLE 13 – DURATION AND TERMINATION
13.1 Duration
This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and remains in effect until terminated by either party.

13.2 Termination by the Artist
The Artist may terminate this Agreement:
(a) At its discretion: upon 30 days' written notice, for any or no reason;
(b) For valid reason (immediate termination): if Platform:
• Commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within 15 days of written notice;
• Files for bankruptcy or becomes insolvent;
• Ceases its activities;
• commits fraud or is grossly negligent.
13.3 Termination by the Platform 
The Platform may terminate this Agreement:
(a) Voluntarily: upon 60 days’ written notice, for any reason whatsoever;
(b) For valid reason (immediate termination): if the Artist:
• commits a material violation of this Agreement (3 violations in the last 12 months);
• uploads prohibited content after receiving a warning;
• Engages in fraud, money laundering or illegal activities;
• Repeatedly infringes the intellectual property rights of third parties (three strikes policy);
• Attempts to circumvent payment systems.
Termination for cause requires written notice specifying the violation and allowing 10 days to remedy the violation, except in the case of illegal activity (immediate termination).
13.4 Effects of termination
In the event of termination:
(a) Unsold works: the artist may request their withdrawal within 30 days; the platform will comply with it within 14 days;
(b) Works sold: licenses granted to buyers remain valid; the platform keeps archive copies;
(c) Pending Payments: All amounts due, collected but not paid, will be paid within 30 days;
(d) Sales in progress: transactions in progress will be completed; final payment will be made within 60 days;
(e) Export of data: the artist can export all data within 90 days;
(f) Promotional materials: the Platform will terminate any new promotions but may retain previous materials for archiving purposes.
13.5 Continuation in force
The following provisions remain in effect after termination: - Section 4.5 (Intellectual Property Rights after Termination) - Section 6.7 (Pending Payments) - Section 10 (Indemnification) - Section 11 (Limitation of Liability) - Section 16 (Confidentiality)
